# Getting started

This guide takes you from download to your first presentation in about five minutes.

## 1. Install

1. Download **[Lectern.zip](https://github.com/tszaks/lectern/releases/latest/download/Lectern.zip)**.
2. Open the zip, and drag **Lectern** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open Lectern from Applications (or Spotlight).

Lectern is signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without a warning. It needs **macOS 12 Monterey or newer** and runs on Apple-silicon and Intel Macs.

## 2. Make a project

Lectern opens to **Home**, your list of projects.

- To present a deck you already have, click **Open…** and choose its HTML file or its folder. Lectern links to it (nothing is copied), so later changes to the deck show up in Lectern.
- To start fresh, click **New project**. The project opens empty. Click **Import a deck folder…**, **Import an HTML file…**, or **Import slide images…**, or drag files onto the window.

> **Tip:** for an HTML deck that uses images or fonts, import the **whole folder**, so its images and fonts come along.

Want to try it right away? Download the repo and open [`examples/welcome`](../examples/welcome).

## 3. Look through your slides

Inside a project:

- slide thumbnails run down the left; click one to go to it,
- the arrow keys move through the slides,
- notes for the current slide sit under the slide; type to add or change them.

## 4. Present

1. Connect the TV or projector (or not, to rehearse).
2. Click **▶ Present** in the top-right corner and choose **Presenter view**.
3. The slides go full screen on the other screen. Your Mac shows the current slide, the next slide, your notes, and a timer.
4. Use the arrow keys or your clicker. Press **Esc** when you are done.
